Hole Munch – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Hole Munch is a casual game made by an independent developer. This policy explains what data the game uses on the web, on YouTube Playables and in the Android app.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, chat, or user-generated content, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Game progress

Your progress (level, coins, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device, or in YouTube's cloud save when you play on YouTube Playables. We cannot see or access it. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes it.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to content rated for general audiences, so ads are not personalized and the app does not access the advertising ID. AdMob may still process limited technical data (such as IP address and device type) to deliver ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

On YouTube Playables, ads are provided by YouTube under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is designed to be safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
