Hole Munch – Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback: email phucpdbk@gmail.com. Please include your device model, OS version and the city or daily game where the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. There is no account or cloud save, so deleting the app or clearing its data removes your progress.

Do I need an internet connection? No. The game plays fully offline.

How do I play? Drag one finger to steer the hole. Swallow small things to grow, then swallow the city's landmark before time runs out.

Privacy: see the privacy policy.
